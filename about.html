About SMASH PLATE

Where Every Bite Is Smash Hit — since 2024.

Our Story

SMASH PLATE was born in 2024 with one simple goal: to serve food that makes people smile. What started as a small kitchen operation has grown into a beloved local brand, known for bold flavours and fast, friendly service.

Every dish we make is prepared fresh, packed with care, and delivered with speed. From our signature smash burgers to our hearty meal packs, everything we do is built around one promise — that every bite really is a smash hit.

We're proud to be a local, community-driven business. Our riders know the streets, our kitchen knows the flavours, and our customers know they can count on us.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver delicious, affordable meals with speed and a smile — every single time. We exist to make great food accessible to everyone in our community.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's favourite local food brand, one smash hit at a time. We want every neighbourhood to know the SMASH PLATE name.

Our Values

Quality

We never compromise on taste or freshness. Every meal is made to order using locally sourced ingredients.

Speed

Fast service without cutting corners. Our riders get your food to you hot and on time.

Community

Proudly local, proudly South African. We support our neighbourhood and grow with the people in it.

Why We're Different

Fresh, Never Frozen

We prepare every meal from scratch. No pre-cooked, no reheated — just fresh food made the way it should be.

Real Local Delivery

Our own SMASH DELIVERY riders handle every order. No third-party apps, no cold food, no hidden fees.

Affordable for Everyone

Meals from just R49. We believe good food shouldn't cost a fortune, so we keep our prices fair and our portions generous.

Made with Care

From the packaging to the presentation, we treat every order like it's going to our own family.
